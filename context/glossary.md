# GarmentPro Business Glossary

- **Team-day:** One production team's recorded performance for a working day; the source data can contain multiple records for a date.
- **SMV:** Standard Minute Value, the expected production time assigned to a garment operation or style.
- **WIP:** Work in progress, the number of garment pieces waiting or undergoing production.
- **Idle time:** Recorded time during which production resources were not actively working.
- **Overtime:** Overtime labor recorded for a team-day, as represented in the source dataset.
- **Style change:** A change in product style that may require setup or a production-line adjustment.
- **Association:** A statistical relationship in the dashboard; it does not establish that a factor caused productivity to change.

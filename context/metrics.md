# GarmentPro Metric Definitions

Replace or extend these examples with the definitions approved by the business team.

## Actual productivity
The observed productivity score for a garment production team-day. Stored as a ratio from 0 to 1; display it as a percentage.

## Targeted productivity
The productivity target assigned to a team-day. Stored as a ratio from 0 to 1; compare actual productivity with this value in percentage points.

## Target achievement
Average actual productivity divided by average targeted productivity. A value of 100% means the overall target was met.

## Below-target rate
The share of observations where actual productivity is less than targeted productivity.
